01 — About

Reading roomssince 2008.

Weddings, corporate evenings and club nights — one ear for all three.

Kathmandu-born and Delhi-raised, Prashant Gautam — Parshh — took his first residency at fifteen, at Royale Mirage, New Delhi. Eighteen years on, his calendar runs from club residencies to five-star weddings and corporate galas across 17+ cities.

What clients book is judgement. Knowing when a sangeet needs the family classics and when it needs the floor-fillers; when a gala should stay elegant and when it is ready to dance. Parshh adapts to every occasion and curates the vibe live — reading the room until it is exactly where it should be.
